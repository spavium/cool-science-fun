SILICON

so what are the physical properties of silicon?

silicon is a mettalic elemennt that is reflective and a              colour

silicon has a melting point of 1414 °C and the boiling point of 3265 °C

it isnt very dense with a density of 2.33 g/cm³

at room temperature it is quite brittle and easy to break but when it is heated to 540°C it become ductile

it is also good at conducting thermal energy and is a semiconductor for electricity

now for the chemical properties

when heated with halogens it reacts quite violently and when it is reacting to something it makes and breaks
covalent bonds but doesnt make or break ionic bonds

it also can be dissolved in acid if the acid is a mix of nitric and hydrofluoric acids
