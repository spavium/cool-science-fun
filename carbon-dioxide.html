CARBON DIOXIDE

carbon dioxide is a colorless and odorless thing that can be a gas, solid and liquid

it has a density of 1.98 kg/m cubed which makes it heavier than air

it is moderatlly soluble in water

and it isnt flameable or electrically or thermally conductive

when it is a solid it is dry ice

and at -56.6°C it is a solid gas and liquid all at the same time

chemical properties

when it is dissolved in water it reacts to create a weak acid called carbonic acid

it is highly reactive with burning magnesium

and when reacting it makes covalent bonds
