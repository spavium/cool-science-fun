SODIUM AZIDE

sodium azide is a colourless and odorless hexagonal crystal

it is soluble in water and it melts at 275°C and undergoes decomposition quickly after melting

sodium azide is only conductive when it is molten or when it is dissolved in water, it isnt good at conducting heat however

and it isnt malleable

chemical properties

when mixed with acid it creates a highly toxic and volatile hydrazoic acid

it does react with heavy metals such as copper and lead
